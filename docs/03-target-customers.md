# 03 · Target Customers

> **Updated:** 2026-06-28 · Inferred from positioning, pricing, locations & messaging. Confirm with client.

---

## Who buys (segments)

### 1. The Weekend Escaper *(primary — emotional)*
Affluent urban professionals/families (Mumbai & Pune, 35–60), who own or can buy a plot in a getaway belt and want a beautiful second home **without** the 2–3 year villa construction nightmare.
- **Wants:** nature, status, a place for memories, *fast*, low-hassle.
- **Trigger:** "I have land / I want land, but building is a pain."

### 2. The Yield Seeker *(primary — rational)*
Investors & landowners who see the pod as an **income asset** — a micro-resort / Airbnb unit on idle land.
- **Wants:** clear ROI, occupancy/rental proof, hands-off management.
- **Trigger:** "My land/villa is sitting idle. Make it pay."

### 3. Hospitality / Resort Developers
Homestay, glamping and boutique-resort operators scaling room count fast and cheaply with modular units.

### 4. Remote Worker / Creative *(Nomad pod persona)*
"Work From Anywhere" professionals wanting a studio/retreat — smaller budget, design-led.

### Secondary: NRIs with ancestral land · wellness/retreat operators · brands wanting unique stays.

---

## Core motivations
- **Speed** — escape in 30–45 days, not years
- **Beauty & escape** — Scandinavian design, nature immersion, "memories now"
- **Smart money** — turn land into yield; multiple finance routes; ROI 1–2 yrs
- **Low hassle** — plug & play, no permissions, AMC-backed
- **Legacy & status** — "owning land is the beginning of a legacy"

## Objections to pre-empt on the site
| Objection | Site answer |
|---|---|
| "Is prefab durable / cheap-feeling?" | 30+ yr lifespan, industrial-grade, "Built to Last", real renders + testimonials |
| "Legal / permissions?" | Temporary structure, no municipal permissions, no FSI, CRZ-1 friendly |
| "Can I afford it?" | EMI / Lease / Revenue-share, "from ₹5L", transparent options |
| "Will it actually earn?" | ROI 1–2 yrs, platform logos (Airbnb/StayVista/Booking), revenue-share |
| "Will my plot work?" | Free site survey; weather-tolerant; ~1,000–2,000 sq ft |
| "After I buy?" | AMC program, handholding, owner community |

## Decision drivers (design implications)
- **Trust & proof** → real photos, built examples, testimonials, specs.
- **Aspiration** → cinematic hero, lifestyle imagery, big emotional lines.
- **Clarity on money** → clean pricing/finance/ROI section.
- **Frictionless contact** → **WhatsApp everywhere** (their preferred, instant, low-commitment channel for Indian buyers).

## Funnel & the WhatsApp role
Cold (scrolling IG/Google) → **Hero hooks emotionally** → pods + proof build desire → ownership/ROI handles the rational buyer → **WhatsApp CTA converts** (low-friction "just ask"). The site's job is **desire + trust → tap WhatsApp**, not to close the sale itself.
