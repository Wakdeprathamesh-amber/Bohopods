# 05 · Brand Identity & Design Direction

> **Updated:** 2026-06-28 · Palette/type **estimated from the brochure** (`media/brochures/NOMAD.pdf`) and live site — confirm/tune with client. This is the design north-star for the new site.

## The aesthetic in one phrase
**Scandinavian-organic luxury** — *not* terracotta-bohemian. Earthy **greens + warm cream**, generous whitespace, cinematic nature photography, and a signature **topographic contour-line** motif. Calm, premium, modern, alive.

## Colour palette (estimated)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Forest (primary/ink) | `#36441F` | Headings, dark text, footer |
| Olive / Sage (brand) | `#8A9A66` | Section bands, buttons, accents |

> Mood: forest-floor + linen + golden hour. High contrast between deep forest green and cream = elegant and legible.

## Typography (recommended — all free, Google Fonts)
| Role | Font | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Display / UI | **Jost** | Geometric humanist sans (Futura-like) — matches the brochure's spaced-out headings & labels |
| Editorial accent | **Cormorant Garamond** (italic) | Elegant high-contrast serif for pull-quotes ("Owning land is the beginning of a legacy.") |
| Script flourish *(rare)* | **Pinyon Script** / Tangerine | Only for occasional flourishes like "Discover the Art of Living" |
| Body | **Inter** (or Mulish) | Clean, legible, neutral |

Headings: wide letter-spacing, often **UPPERCASE** for pod names (N O M A D, G A T S B Y).

## Signature visual motifs
- **Topographic contour lines** — the hallmark. Use as faint background texture, section dividers, hero overlays.
- **Dashed circular badges / outline circles** — for value-prop icons & stats.
- **Hairline rules** + lots of negative space.
- **Alternating sage / cream bands** to segment the long scroll.
- **Full-bleed cinematic imagery** with overlaid text; subtle **film grain**.
- **Pod card pattern:** big number → spaced NAME → short tagline → 1 short paragraph → CTA (straight from the brochure layout — reuse it).

## Photography / video direction
- Golden-hour & blue-hour, soft mist, cinematic depth.
- Pods integrated into drama: cliffs over the sea, forested hills, beaches, ghats.
- Glass reflections; **indoor↔outdoor continuity** (the "inside but outside" idea).
- Aerial micro-resort shots (many pods on a hillside) for the ownership/scale story.
- Lifestyle: fire pit, coffee on the deck, reading by glass walls.
- Consistent warm, natural colour grade across all assets.

## Do / Don't
- ✅ Calm, airy, cinematic, premium. Let imagery breathe.
- ✅ Motion that feels effortless (slow, eased, intentional).
- ❌ No terracotta/"hippie boho" clichés, no clutter, no neon, no heavy drop-shadows.
- ❌ Don't bury the nature photography behind UI.
