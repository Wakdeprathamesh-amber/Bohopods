## ✅ Where to upload WHAT

| You have… | Put it in… |
|---|---|
| **Gatsby exterior photos** (golden hour, deck, dusk-lit, wide shots) | `media/hero-gatsby/exterior/` |
| **Gatsby interior photos** (living, bedroom, courtyard, skylight, details) | `media/hero-gatsby/interior/` |
| **Gatsby video** (if you have/make one) | `media/hero-gatsby/video/` |
| Photos of **any other pod** (Nomad, Curv, Flo, Dojopod, EcoPod…) | `media/pods/<pod-name>/` e.g. `media/pods/nomad/` |
| **Logo** (best as `.svg`; else high-res `.png`), brand fonts, brand guide | `media/brand/` |
| Any **PDF / brochure / flip-book export** | `media/brochures/` |
| **Real built-pod / customer / location** photos | `media/pods/<name>/` or `media/brand/real-builds/` |

> If unsure, just drop everything into `media/hero-gatsby/` (Gatsby) or `media/brand/` (everything else) and tell me — I'll organise it.

## 🎯 Priority order (what unblocks the hero fastest)
1. **Gatsby exterior + interior photos** → I can build the hero immediately (parallax now, video next).
2. **Vector logo (SVG)** → crisp logo in nav/hero/footer.
3. Other pod photos → so each model card shows the right pod.
4. Real built photos → trust section.

## 📐 File specs (don't worry too much — send the best you have)
- **Send the highest resolution / original quality you have** — I downscale & convert; I can't add detail back.
- Photos: ≥ 2400px on the long edge ideal (JPG/PNG/HEIC all fine).
- Video: ≥ 1080p, horizontal; raw clips are fine — I'll edit/compress.
- Naming: anything works; *nice-to-have* = lowercase, descriptive, e.g. `gatsby-exterior-dusk-deck.jpg`.

## 🔄 What I do with your uploads
Curate the best → optimise (WebP/AVIF, responsive sizes, compression) → place into the build → keep your originals untouched in `media/`.
