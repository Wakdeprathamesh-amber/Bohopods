# 12 · Design Director's Cut — Enhance / Add / Subtract

> Fresh-eyes review of the **current build** (post P1+P2 fixes from [docs/11](11-change-and-enhancement-report.md)).
> Frame: the site is now polished (~8.8/10). The gap to *amazing* is **editing, tempo and humanity** — great design is as much about what you remove as what you add.
> **Updated:** 2026-06-28

---

## The one-paragraph verdict
The bones are excellent: cinematic Gatsby hero, disciplined Scandinavian-organic system, strong funnel, three genuinely good infographics, WhatsApp-everywhere. What's holding it back now: **the homepage still says everything twice** (18 benefit bullets across three list sections), **everything lives in identical rounded cards** (flattens hierarchy), the **emotional register is even** (no peaks), and it's still **uninhabited** (no people/proof — asset-dependent). Amazing = fewer, harder-hitting sections + 2–3 statement moments + humans.

---

## ➖ SUBTRACT (highest-value lens now)

| # | Cut / tighten | Why |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Merge About into Why Boho** — one brand moment, not two | Two "who we are" sections dilute each other; the craft cards (factory/India-engineered/light-on-land) can live inside Why Boho as a second row. Saves a full section. |
| S2 | **Demote Product Advantage** — keep the stat band; compress the 6 advantage items into a slim icon strip (one line each) or fold into FAQ/pod pages | The page currently carries **Why Boho (6) + Advantage (6) + Comparison (7v7)** = ~18 benefit statements. Diminishing returns; each list weakens the others. Keep Comparison (it *reframes*), keep pillars (they're the concept), thin the middle. |
| S3 | **Compress Experiences** to a slim "Stay in one first" banner (1 line + 3 price chips + CTA) attached to Ownership | Clever argument, but it's a supporting point, not a chapter. A banner keeps the idea at 20% of the height. |
| S4 | **Reduce card-chrome globally** — borders/cards only where interactive (pod cards) or data (ownership options) | Right now pillars, advantages, ownership, experiences, testimonials, craft values are *all* rounded-bordered cards → visual monotony, weak hierarchy. Subtracting boxes is the fastest premium-feel win. |
| S5 | **De-duplicate "30–45 days"** (appears ~6×: hero, pillars, comparison, stats, process, FAQ) | Keep 3 mentions (hero, comparison, process); vary the framing elsewhere ("this season", "weeks not years"). Repetition should feel like a drumbeat, not a stuck record. |

**Net effect:** homepage 16 → **~12 sections**, every remaining section hits harder, scroll fatigue drops.

## ✨ ENHANCE (make what exists sing)

| # | Enhancement | Why |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Create 2–3 "statement moments"** — e.g. the Why Boho headline at hero-scale type; one full-bleed image break (a single stunning render, no text) between Comparison and Ownership | The page has even intensity throughout; memory is made by peaks (peak-end rule). Tempo: loud → quiet → loud. |
| E2 | **Redesign testimonials as editorial pull-quotes** — large Cormorant italic, no cards, generous whitespace, alternating alignment | Kills card monotony (S4), reads premium/editorial, and quotes deserve typographic celebration. Add faces when client supplies. |
| E3 | **Pod-page gallery → mixed grid + lightbox** — first image spans 2 columns, click to zoom | The gallery is now correctly placed (position 3); make it *feel* like a gallery, not a grid of thumbnails. |
| E4 | "Explore the range" → **prefer same-family pods** (Dojopod ↔ Gazepod ↔ Quad family) | Smarter up/down-sell; relevance beats randomness. |
| E5 | **Micro-interactions:** arrow slides 2px on CTA hover · nav active state · count-up stats · slideshow slide-dots (tiny, bottom-left) | The final 5% of perceived quality lives here. All cheap. |
| E6 | **Mobile menu upgrade** — add phone + WhatsApp buttons side-by-side and IG link inside the menu | Mobile users convert by *calling* too; don't bury the phone. |
| E7 | **Branded 404** — a misty pod render + "Looks like you've wandered off the trail" + CTA home | Tiny, delightful, on-voice. Currently default Next 404. |
| E8 | **Scroll-progress hairline** (top of viewport, olive) | Orientation on a long page without the weight of a dot-nav. |

## ➕ ADD (genuine gaps)

| # | Addition | Why | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **"Make it yours" add-ons strip** — plunge pool, jacuzzi, solar, outdoor kitchen, fire pit… (real content from the live site's add-ons list) | We currently never mention add-ons — a real desire-builder + upsell the live site has and we dropped. One horizontal scroller of 6–8 chips/cards. | `[Now]` |
| A2 | **People & real-build photography** woven into hero #3 slot, About/Why Boho, Gallery, testimonials | Still the #1 emotional + trust gap. The site is beautiful but uninhabited. | `[Asset]` |
| A3 | **Hard proof numbers** in the trust bar ("Since 20XX · N pods delivered · M destinations") | Converts the trust bar from association → evidence. | `[Asset]` |
| A4 | **Gatsby walkthrough MP4** replacing the slideshow when ready | The one asset that would out-punch everything. | `[Asset]` |
| A5 | *(Phase 2)* Channel-partner mini page · per-location SEO landing pages · stays/booking page | Mirrors live-site content we haven't ported; compounding SEO. | Later |

## Priority order
1. **S1–S5 subtractions** + **E1 statement moments** + **E2 testimonial redesign** *(one combined homepage-edit pass — biggest taste jump, zero assets)*
2. **A1 add-ons strip** + **E5 micro-interactions** + **E7 404** + **E3 lightbox**
3. **A2–A4** the moment assets arrive.
