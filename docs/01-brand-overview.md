## In one line
Boho Pods designs and sells **plug-n-play prefab cabins & pods with Scandinavian design** — premium modular escapes installed in **30–45 days**, on your own land, ready to live in *or* rent out.

## Signature messaging (use these)
- **SELECT · INSTALL · STAY** — the core 3-step promise / brand ribbon
- *"inside but outside but still inside"* — glass-walled, nature-immersed living
- *"Why wait years to build your villa when you can start making memories now"*
- *"Owning land is the beginning of a legacy."*
- *"Discover the Art of Living, One Pod at a Time"*

## Brand story (verbatim — brochure)
> "We believe your land should start giving back to you the moment you own it. That's why we design and craft prefab cabins and pods that let you step into comfort, beauty, and freedom—without the wait. Inspired by Scandinavian simplicity and infused with modern functionality, our pods are more than spaces; they're experiences. Whether you're seeking a weekend retreat, a creative studio, or a bridge to your dream villa, our cabins create the perfect balance between **nature and nurture**."

## Positioning — two stories in one
1. **The dream** — a beautiful, fast, low-hassle nature escape (weekend retreat / creative studio / bridge to your dream villa).
2. **The asset** — a high-yield rental that turns idle land into income (Airbnb / StayVista / Booking.com).

Premium yet accessible · eco-conscious · design-led · modern. Speaks to both **heart** (escape) and **head** (ROI).

## Why Choose Us — 6 pillars (brochure)
1. Thoughtful Design That Speaks to You
2. From Idea to Escape in Just 30–45 Days
3. Modular by Nature, Flexible by Design
4. Smart Ownership, Made Accessible
5. A Community of Modern Pioneers
6. Built to Last, Inside and Out

## Voice & tone
Aspirational, warm, confident, lightly poetic but always clear. Short, sensory lines (*serenity, harmony, nature & nurture, memories, legacy*). Premium — never discount-y or jargon-heavy.

## Key facts at a glance
- **12+ pod models** (₹5L compact → ₹95L 3-BHK) + stay experiences
- Install in **30–45 days** · **30+ yr lifespan** · plug & play · AMC-backed · weather-tolerant
- **No municipal permissions** · temporary structure (no FSI impact) · CRZ-1 friendly · low carbon
- Finance: Outright (20:20:20:20) · EMI · Lease · Revenue-share (50:50 / 70:30) · **ROI in 1–2 yrs**
