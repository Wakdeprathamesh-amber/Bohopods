## HQ

## Locations served (getaway belts)
Concentrated in **Maharashtra + nearby leisure destinations** — i.e. 2–6 hrs from Mumbai/Pune:

**Maharashtra:** Lonavala · Khandala · Alibaug · Mahabaleshwar · Panchgani · Nashik · Igatpuri · Karjat · Manor · Palghar · Gorai · Madh Island · Vasai · Pune (outskirts)
**Coastal / leisure:** Goa
**Hill stations (wider):** Coorg (Karnataka) · Kodaikanal (Tamil Nadu)

> These are classic **second-home / weekend-getaway markets** for Mumbai & Pune money. The pattern: scenic land within a short drive of a metro.

## Why this geography matters for the build
- **Climate range** → "weather tolerance" is a real selling point (coastal humidity & salt, ghats monsoon, inland heat). Feature it.
- **Coastal CRZ zones** → "can be placed in CRZ-1 with nominal permissions" is a genuine differentiator near beaches — call it out for Alibaug/Goa buyers.
- **Site/plot suitability** → buyers ask "will it work on my land?" → free site survey CTA.

## Implications for the website
- A **Locations** section: "Where will yours stand?" with the key belts (Lonavala, Alibaug, Goa, Coorg, Mahabaleshwar…) as evocative cards/map — aspirational, not a literal store-locator.
- **Local SEO**: target geo + product keywords — *prefab cabins India*, *glamping pods Lonavala / Alibaug / Goa*, *modular farmhouse Maharashtra*, *Airbnb pod investment India*.
- **NAP consistency** (Name/Address/Phone) in footer + JSON-LD `LocalBusiness` schema.
- **Currency/format**: ₹ Lakhs, Indian audience, English (India). WhatsApp is the dominant contact norm.
