## Product advantages (brochure "Product Advantage")
- **Temporary Structure** — doesn't count toward FSI; minimal permissions
- **30+ Year Lifespan** — industrial-grade materials
- **AMC Program** — annual maintenance contract / after-sales
- **Plug & Play** — arrives ready; quick on-site install
- **Weather Tolerance** — engineered for Indian extremes (e.g. Rajasthan summers, Karnataka monsoons)

Plus (site): no municipal permissions · low maintenance · low carbon footprint · can sit in CRZ-1 with nominal permissions.

## Buying options (brochure "Buying Options — Three Options")
1. **Outright Purchase** — 20:20:20:20 spread across the ~5-week build
2. **EMI** — from ₹5L down · 6 or 12 months (subject to CIBIL approval)
3. **Lease Program** — ₹5L down + ₹50k/month · 5-yr lock-in · for micro-resorts/homestays

### Revenue / ownership models
- **Revenue Share 50** — ₹5L down · 50:50 split with company
- **Revenue Share 70** — ₹7L down · 70:30 split
- **Pod Owner Barter Program** — pan-India free stays for owners (coming soon)
- **The pitch:** *"With only ~2,000 sq. ft., a Boho Pod can transform idle space into a high-yield rental that pays for upkeep, staffing, and generates additional income."* ROI in **1–2 years** via Airbnb, StayVista, SaffronStays, Booking.com, Bonvoy Cabins.
